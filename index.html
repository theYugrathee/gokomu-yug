How to Play

Objective

Be the first player to form an unbroken line of five stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Gameplay

  • Players take turns placing stones on the board
  • Black plays first, followed by White
  • Stones are placed on intersections of the grid lines
  • Once placed, stones are not moved or removed

Winning

Help

Controls

Mobile: Tap on the intersection where you want to place your stone

Desktop: Click on the intersection where you want to place your stone

Game Features

  • Undo: Take back your last move
  • New Game: Start a fresh game
  • Pass & Play: Two players sharing the same device

Tips

  • Try to create multiple threats at once
  • Block your opponent's attempts to form five in a row
  • Control the center of the board for more flexibility

About Gomoku YUG

Gomoku YUG, also known as Five in a Row, is a traditional strategy board game that originated in China. It's played on a 15×15 grid where players attempt to get five pieces in a row while blocking their opponent.
